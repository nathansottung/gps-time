# Code of conduct

GPS Time follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion about the work, and don't harass anyone. Don't post anyone's location, address or other personal details, including your own station's exact position.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)), or by a direct message to @nathansottung on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues, or block contributors, who break this code.
