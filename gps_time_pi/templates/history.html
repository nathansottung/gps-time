Clock offset and uncertainty

The line is chrony's estimated system-clock offset; the shaded band is its reported uncertainty. Watch the band collapse after boot as the clock walks into true time, balloon during holdover, and snap shut on PPS reacquisition. The vertical scale is symmetric-log: linear within ±1 µs, logarithmic beyond.

Data source: chrony's own tracking log (log tracking in chrony.conf), one record per clock update. The history survives restarts of this page because chrony owns the file.
