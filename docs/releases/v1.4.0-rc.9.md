# GPS Time v1.4.0-rc.9

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Type:** pre-release (release candidate) · **Previous:** [v1.4.0-rc.8](v1.4.0-rc.8.md)

Ninth release candidate, and the bundle the deploy checklists target. It fixes what the 14-day rc.8 soak run exposed, and is the candidate for the next soak run before v1.4.0.

Source: `docs/provenance/change-log.md`, entries after v1.4.0-rc.8 (#60 to #76).

## What the rc.8 soak run found

v1.4.0 was not tagged. Over 14 days 20 h on the Pi, the station had real PPS/GNSS outages on 2026-10-17 (ten events between 19:04 and 20:35 UTC, about 71 minutes). chrony coasted through a 56-minute gap without switching source and was +1.68 µs off when PPS returned. The system journal had nothing about it. The run also showed a whole-file log copy to the SD card every hour, a PPS missed-pulse count of about 9 % that the outages don't explain, and a browser round trip of seconds. Record: `docs/development/raspberry-pi-deploy.md` → "Soak run of v1.4.0-rc.8".

## Fixes from the soak run

- **Tracking-log flush with caps** (#72; GPS-011, GPS-012): the hourly flush appends only the new lines to `/var/log/chrony/station-tracking.txt` instead of copying the whole RAM log (about 240 MB a day at the check). RAM log capped at 8 MiB and the SD copy at 16 MiB, one previous file each; the SD copy is out of logrotate's reach. The old full copy ages out.
- **Outage evidence on the SD card** (#73; OBS §13): one file per outage with the event, a 10 s timeline of the station's view and chrony's tracking lines from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after. Written 5 minutes after the outage opens and again after it closes; 20 MiB cap; no position. Listed on Timing → PPS Events.
- **PPS reads on their own thread** (#70): no slow evaluation or HTTP request can delay reading a pulse. Missed pulses are still counted from the kernel's sequence number; pulses the kernel counted but the station didn't read one by one are now counted separately ("unread"), with poll statistics. The next soak run shows which the 9 % was. Outage detection is unchanged.
- **Browser round trip** (#74): measured on the Pi, `/api/ping` alone took about 10 ms but waited behind `/api/almanac` (about 15 s of CPU, requested every 5 s by the legacy dashboard). The almanac's day-level work is now cached and concurrent identical requests share one computation; `/api/references` sends one server's history instead of about 1 MB; the history endpoint parses the log once a minute.

## New

- **Oscillator log** (#71): the SoC temperature logged with chrony's frequency in 10-minute bins, appended to `oscillator-log.csv` once an hour (4 MB cap, bound to the board by a hash of its serial number). Timing → Oscillator shows frequency against temperature and a straight-line slope. It is the data for the trained oscillator profile, which is not built yet.
- **Earth shadow and satellites** (#61; Addendum §1–§5): umbra/penumbra model, zenith shadow height, illumination sky map, contamination summary; satellites from an offline TLE cache filled by `python -m gps_time_pi.tle_fetch` (the web service never fetches). Sun & Astronomy → Earth Shadow and Satellites.
- **Sunrise, noon and sunset shifts in h/m/s** on the Overview (#75).
- Docs: the RF node / TDOA spec (PLANNED, #60), the legacy-removal plan (nothing removed, #62).
